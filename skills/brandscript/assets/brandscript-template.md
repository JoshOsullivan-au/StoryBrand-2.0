# StoryBrand Framework: Template

Blank client worksheet. `brandscript.py show --format template` prints this layout filled from a stored BrandScript. The field each line maps to is in brackets. Remove the brackets before a client sees the sheet.

## 1. A character
Who is your customer, and what do they want?

* Our customer is: [character.customer]
* They want: [character.want]

## 2. Has a problem
What prevents them from getting what they want?

* Villain, the root cause: [problem.villain]
* External problem, the practical obstacle: [problem.external]
* Internal problem, how it makes them feel: [problem.internal]
* Philosophical problem, why this should not be this way: [problem.philosophical]

## 3. Meets a guide
Why should they trust your business to help?

* Empathy, how we demonstrate understanding: [guide.empathy]

## 8. Controlling idea
What is this story about? The one point to repeat everywhere. (Building a StoryBrand 2.0, chapter 12)

* Controlling idea: [controlling_idea]

## Combined narrative

Our customer, [character.customer], wants [character.want].
But [problem.villain] stands in their way, leaving them feeling [problem.internal]. They believe [problem.philosophical].
We understand [guide.empathy] and have demonstrated our ability to help through [guide.authority].
Our plan is simple: [step 1], [step 2], then [step 3].
We invite them to [cta.direct].
By acting, they can avoid [failure] and achieve [success], moving from [identity.from] to [identity.to].

## Writing each field so the narrative reads

| Field | Write it as | Example |
|---|---|---|
| character.customer | A plural noun phrase | sole-trader tradies |
| character.want | What they want, no verb | books that are always BAS-ready |
| problem.villain | A noun phrase | the shoebox of receipts |
| problem.internal | A feeling, after "feeling" | behind and anxious about every BAS deadline |
| problem.philosophical | A belief with should or deserve | a good tradie should not lose weekends to paperwork |
| guide.empathy | Starts "We understand" or "We know" | We know the dread of a BAS letter on a Friday |
| plan.process.steps | Imperative, short | Book a 15-minute call |
| cta.direct | Imperative, after "invite them to" | book a call |
| failure, success | Noun phrases | ATO penalties; calm at tax time |
| identity.from, identity.to | Adjective phrases | behind and anxious; on top of the numbers |
| controlling_idea | One complete, simple line a customer can repeat | Your weekends should be yours, not your receipts' |
